Terms of sale

What you are buying

Every item in this library is a digital study document, delivered as a PDF by email. Nothing physical is shipped. Prices are shown in Indian rupees and include any applicable taxes unless stated otherwise on the item.

How an order works

Payment is taken by Razorpay — UPI, cards, netbanking or wallets, whichever you choose on their payment page. We never see or store your card, bank or UPI details; they are entered directly with Razorpay, not with us. The moment Razorpay confirms your payment to us, delivery is automatic: your download link appears on the page you're returned to, and is emailed to you as well. Nobody has to manually check or release an order.

An order reference beginning DV is generated when you start checkout, before you pay. It helps us find your order — quote it in any email to us — but proof of payment itself lives with Razorpay, against whichever payment method you used.

What you may and may not do with the notes

Buying a title gives you a personal, non-transferable licence to use it for your own study. You may print it and annotate it for your own use.

  • Do not resell, share, upload or redistribute the files.
  • Do not post them to file-sharing groups, drives or channels.
  • Do not present the material as your own work.

Copyright in the notes remains with the author. We may withdraw access and decline future orders where these terms are broken.

Availability and changes

Titles, prices and content may change. Notes are revised as syllabuses and guidelines change; the version you receive is the current one at the time of your order. We do not guarantee free updates to a title after purchase, although we often provide them.

Refund policy

Digital notes cannot be returned once they have been delivered, so refunds are limited to the situations below. We would rather fix a problem than keep money for something that did not work.

You will get a full refund if

  • You paid but never received the files, and we cannot deliver them.
  • The file is corrupt, unreadable, or is not the title you ordered, and we cannot supply a working replacement.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order.

Refunds are not normally given if

  • The notes were delivered as described and you changed your mind.
  • You bought the wrong title but the correct one was clearly listed — though tell us anyway, and we will usually swap it.
  • You expected content that was not described on the product page.

How to ask

Email the contact address in the footer with your order reference within 7 days of delivery. We aim to respond within 3 working days. Approved refunds are issued back to the original payment method through Razorpay, and usually clear within 5 to 7 working days.

Privacy

What stays in your browser

Your cart, your saved profile and your order history are stored in this browser's local storage on your own device. They are not sent to us, and we cannot read them. Clearing your browser data, or using the Clear local data button on the profile page, removes them permanently.

What we actually receive

We receive the email address and titles you check out with, your order's status, and — once Razorpay confirms a payment — the payment ID it generates for that transaction. We use it to deliver your files and answer questions about your order. We do not receive or store your card number, bank details or UPI ID; those go directly to Razorpay.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts run on this site.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your details with anyone beyond the service providers below, and only for the purpose of completing your order.
  • We never see or store your card, bank or UPI credentials — payment is entered directly with Razorpay, not with us.

Third parties

Checkout necessarily involves a small number of service providers who each handle only what they need to do their one job:

  • Razorpay — processes your payment. Your card, bank and UPI details go to them, never to us.
  • Neon — stores the order record itself (your email, what you bought, its status).
  • Vercel Blob — stores the note files and generates the expiring link you download them from.
  • Resend — sends the order and delivery emails.

None of them may use your data for anything beyond processing your order. Everything else this site needs — fonts, the 3D model, and the libraries that render it — is served from this domain, so browsing the site itself, before you check out, makes no request to any of these companies.

Keeping and deleting your data

Order emails are kept as long as we need them for accounting and support. You can ask us to delete your details at any time using the contact address in the footer, and we will do so unless we are required to keep a record of the transaction.
